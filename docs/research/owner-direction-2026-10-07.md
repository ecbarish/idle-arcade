# Owner direction: scope, platforms, budget and automation

Evan, 2026-10-07; recorded by Codex from his explicit feedback. This is current owner direction, not a research recommendation. It supersedes earlier planning assumptions where they conflict. It does not change released gameplay, authorize a port of every game or silently start a parked implementation.

## Every game belongs in the discussion

Otherworld and Diamond Career/sports were deferred under the earlier finish-and-launch-first decision, not rejected as ideas. They may be researched, discussed and scoped alongside the existing games. Do not interpret “parked implementation” as “do not mention or think about it.” Large parallel builds still need a clear task, ownership and a small playable milestone.

Evan likes Starfall Guild and wants it treated as a worthwhile game in its own right. Primordial currently interests him less; it fills a niche, but that is not a requirement to spend substantial effort on it. Do not maintain equal development effort just to fill portfolio slots, and do not remove the game based on this note.

The next research pack includes separate Otherworld and Diamond Career briefs. Their first-life/first-contract slices should be discussed before extensive systems. The present question about neglect is not itself an instruction to implement all their stages.

## Keep platform choices open

This is a passion project, not a commitment to browser games or to Godot. The installed Godot trial is encouraging evidence about Wildbond's presentation, not a permanent engine choice for the whole portfolio. Learn from each prototype and change direction when evidence supports it. A different engine or browser-based approach can be appropriate for a different game.

Compare the same small experience, not feature lists: quality of play, readable presentation, controls, animation, accessibility, save safety, iteration effort, testing, target devices and ongoing cost. Preserve working code/content and the browser reference while evaluating alternatives. There is no need to build duplicate full ports to keep an open mind.

## Money and effort

Evan wants his principal investment to be time and AI prompting tokens. His current approximate total project cash ceiling is **US$200**, unless he revisits it. This is a budget constraint, not permission to spend it or a reason to invent a shopping list. Prefer existing tools and freely usable assets. Track any actual costs and evaluate total/recurring costs before recommending a paid dependency. No new service subscription or speculative asset purchase is necessary now.

## Automation serves play

Evan's Wildbond play changed his opinion. Full unattended progression is **not a required capability for every game**, and should not replace the meaningful activity the game is designed around. Earlier plans requiring every game to reach full Auto are superseded.

- Quality of life and conveniences are earned/unlocked as appropriate to the game, rather than offered as a way to bypass the journey from the beginning.
- Auto itself may be earned where it has a useful, limited role. It is not forbidden wholesale and is not the default design target.
- Good candidates remove repetition: constant clicking, routine sorting, repetitive travel along known routes, and routine management the player has already learned.
- Preserve deliberate decisions and their feedback: first discoveries, bonding, story choices, interesting battles, exploration and preparation. Judge each mechanic by what the player actually enjoys doing.
- Strategic/management games can naturally let characters execute orders. In Starfall, watching a prepared party act is different from letting an autopilot make every recruitment, preparation and season decision.
- An action can begin as enjoyable learning and later become repetitive; unlock assistance when that change makes sense. Avoid making the player prove mastery through hundreds of meaningless clicks.
- Accessibility assistance is distinct from a progression convenience. Do not require earning the ability to use essential input, readable text, reduced motion or other access features.

This updates future design policy. Existing modes, rewards and saves remain unchanged until a scoped implementation ticket reviews them. Do not remove current player options or rewrite balance simply by applying this document.
