# Wildbond final reveals: decisions before the last page

Codex, 2026-10-09. Writer proposal, **not canon or player dialogue**. WB4.4b remains pending. The ledger says:
"The final truth must be chosen before its reveal is written, and must fit every clue already placed."
[Source ledger](../lore/wildbond-threads.md); [usable league handoff](../lore/wildbond-finale-brief.md).

## Recommended order

Let the league conclude the badge journey and friendship now. Resolve the deeper story after the player discovers
the wild bond, rather than using a title ceremony as a lecture about a power the player has never experienced.
Give the four heritage accounts shared confirming evidence before the common-root reveal; let a corrected
Unbound account distinguish an observed joining from its mistaken interpretation of why the land faded.
This order is staging advice, not a new game mode, date lock or choice of culprit.

Before writing the full reveal, Evan and Claude need a coherent account of these linked questions:

| Decision | Candidates already in the ledger | Writer recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| What did the old bond fight? | Outside memory-feeder; turned guardian; exploiting older order; bond went too far | Choose one before adding any late clue that rules the others out; no default culprit selected here |
| Who were the old pair and what happened? | Both lost; creature survives as guardian; tamer becomes first Warden; pair still somewhere | Make their fate fit the chosen fight, surviving traces and witness directions; don't invent names to settle it |
| Who is Toren's watcher? | Sleeping opponent; creature from old bond; ashamed guardian | Decide jointly with the first two; a warm pocket is not proof of an identity |
| What does the present Unbound leader want? | Sincere belief; power; unknowingly finishing old evil; badly preventing it | Preserve legitimate concern for creatures even if a leader is wrong; do not turn every visitor into a villain |
| Which smaller questions stay open? | Veilmote's guarded nests; Tobin's earlier sighting | Leave one deliberate future hook, clearly distinguished from a forgotten payoff |

Already decided: the wild bond is real; its founder witness misread the fight; the fading took colour and depth;
the four heritages descend from one older people and witnessed different portions of one event. None of those
facts needs another vote. The missing part is an internally consistent final account and the fair evidence that
allows a player to discover it, not a new list of powers or a surprise reversal of established canon.

## Evidence test for the chosen account

For every proposed revelation, cite the ferry tally, carved steps, warm pocket, shelter ties, founding copy,
incomplete shadow record and heritage accounts it explains. Mark what remains ordinary care or a separate small
mystery. Lay out three shared late observations before the reveal; optional heritage/choice variants can change
perspective, never access to the truth. Keep lost pair, watcher, mooring light and bell answers distinct until
evidence deliberately connects them. Narration cannot knowingly mislead.
