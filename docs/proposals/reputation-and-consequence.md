# Hero, nobody or villain: reputation and a world that remembers

Evan, 2026-10-08: in some or all games, decisions can make you anything from a hero to a nobody to a villain, and good
and bad decisions change not only how people treat you but **the world itself**. Otherworld: you destroy a town and
it's gone, or they rebuild and people know it was you (protecting it, or careless). Realmbound: you fought to help
people, or you were greedy, or you made things worse. Wildbond: you join a villain team ("Team Rocket"), or you're the
reason it falls, or you become a double agent in disguise, infiltrate their base, and find things that make you angry,
or make you understand them and wonder if they're right.

This grows VISION §9 (choices that matter). Claude's plan; the Wildbond faction's premise needs Evan's yes.

## Shared rules for every game that uses it

- **Two measures, not one bar.** *Renown* (how known you are: a nobody to a legend) and *standing* (what people think
  of what you did: hero to villain). A famous villain, an unknown helper and a celebrated hero are all real outcomes.
  Standing can differ by place and group (loved in one town, hated in another).
- **The world changes, physically, in the game window:** buildings burned or rebuilt, shops that close or prosper,
  people who leave or move in, guards who stop you, children who copy you, wanted posters, songs at the inn, new routes
  and lost ones. Not a number in a menu; people say it and places show it.
- **Choices aren't labelled.** No "good" and "evil" buttons. You choose what you'd do; people judge it by what happened
  and why (protecting the town while it burned is different from being careless).
- **Every path is a full game.** The villain or double-agent path has its own story, friends and rewards; it is never
  just a punishment. Some doors close on every path.
- **Remembered per save** (one world-state record per save, VISION §9), and some outcomes are permanent.
- **Recoverable but not erasable:** you can make amends and people can forgive, but the world remembers what happened.

## Wildbond: a faction with a point of view

**Proposal (needs Evan's yes):** the valley's open question is why the colour faded (docs/lore/wildbond.md leaves it
deliberately unexplained). A group, working name **the Unbound**, believes **bonding is what drained the colour**: that
tamers taking creatures from their places weakened the land's memory, and that creatures should be freed and the
Wardens' league ended. They "release" ranch creatures, block routes, raid Warden trials, and run a hidden base.

- **They are partly right, and partly wrong.** As you learn more, some of what they say checks out (old records,
  things the guardians show you), and some of their leaders are using the cause for something else. Whether they are
  villains or a warning depends on what you find and what you do.
- **Your paths:** fight them and be the reason they fall; join them (your ranch, your rival Wren and the Wardens react);
  or **infiltrate**: wear their uniform (drawn on your own figure, so the disguise is visible and can be seen through),
  pass their tests, walk their base, and decide whether to take it down, change it from inside, or stay.
- **What it changes:** routes and towns (a town they freed from overhunting thanks you; a ranch they raided does not),
  how Wardens treat you, Wren's story with you, which guardians trust you, and the ending of the colour's return.
- **Fits the wild bond:** their founder may be the last person to have seen the old fusion, which is part of why they
  fear bonding.

## Realmbound: what kind of adventurer you are

- **Quest choices** with real alternatives: return the stolen goods or keep them, spare or kill, take the bribe, burn
  the granary to stop the plague or find another way. Rewards differ; nothing is free.
- **Towns and camps change** (ties to V7 nodes): a village you defended grows; one you robbed or abandoned declines,
  prices rise, guards turn you away, and bounty hunters may come.
- **Your guild's reputation** follows its members: guild members' moods and stories react to what you do.
- Factions already exist (Concord, Wildclan); standing can rise or fall with each, and a villainous path can open
  doors the honest one can't.

## Otherworld: worlds that remember across lives

The best fit of all: **when you are reborn into the same world, it remembers your earlier life.** Lanthorn you burned
is a ruin, or rebuilt with a memorial; people tell stories of "the stranger who..." about you; your past self is a
legend, a ghost story or a warning. Within a life, the living-Lanthorn plan (O1: food and fear over the days) is the
first piece: what you do pushes the town toward hope or panic, prosperity or ruin.

## Diamond Career (lighter)

Clubhouse and public reputation: a leader, a quiet professional, or a hothead; sportsmanship or trash talk; honest
or caught cutting corners. Teammates, coaches, fans and contracts react. No world destruction; a smaller, personal
version of the same idea.
