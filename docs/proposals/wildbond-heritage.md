# Proposal: heritages for Wildbond's tamer (Evan's idea, 2026-10-08)

Evan: having different races and abilities in Wildbond "could be cool. It would add another layer and make the player
feel more connected to their character." **Approved by Evan, 2026-10-08: option A, heritages** ("this sounds similar to the way Baldur's Gate 3 might work":
your origin gives a small edge and changes how people react). To be built in the Godot version after the opening is
finished (QUEUE B2b).

## Fitting it to the world

The lore (docs/lore/wildbond.md) makes people **newcomers**: Larkhaven is a small human foothold beside a much older
land, and the guardians were there first. So two ways in:

- **A. Heritages among the valley's people (recommended).** Everyone is human, but you choose where your family comes
  from, and that shapes your look and one small gift: **Larkhaven farmfolk** (creatures of field and forest warm to you
  a little sooner), **Saltmarsh coastfolk** (water creatures; you read the tide), **Emberfall highlanders** (fire and
  stone creatures; hot springs don't scare you), **Farwatch wanderers** (you find more in the tall grass). Each area's
  people greet you differently ("one of ours" at home, a little wary elsewhere), and Maren's register asks it.
- **B. Other peoples entirely** (not human). Bigger and more striking, but it changes the canon (who lived here before)
  and every character's lines; better kept for later or another game.

## Rules so it stays fair

- Gifts touch **bonding and exploring, never battle strength**, and are small (a few percent, or a different line),
  so no heritage is the best one and none is a trap.
- It is chosen at the register (the character creator), shown in full words, and can be changed once at Maren's.
- Old saves get the Larkhaven heritage.

## Grown (Evan, 2026-10-08, later): heritages as beliefs, starts and roots

Evan: each heritage has its own story or belief about what happened, which changes how your character sees and hears
things; heritage changes the starting experience and is the **first choice** you make; heritages differ in how they
bond (one believes in bonding with a single creature, and so on); and we learn that all the heritages have roots in
different ancient things, their separate beliefs coming from different understandings of the same past.

**The first choice.** Heritage comes before the partner: a short opening in your family's home (V1 prologues), then the
road to Larkhaven and Maren's register. The story meets in Larkhaven; how you arrive, who you know and what you've been
told differ.

**Each heritage: a way of bonding, and a tale of the fading.** (Working names; the tales are deliberately partial.)

| Heritage | How they bond | What they say about the fading |
|---|---|---|
| Larkhaven farmfolk | **Many, as family:** a full ranch, care and routine; the ranch and breeding come naturally to them | The land grew tired after people took too much; care brings it back (fits candidate c in the thread ledger) |
| Saltmarsh coastfolk | **One for life:** a single partner, chosen once, who grows closer and stronger than any other (the one-creature path; the wild bond comes most naturally here) | Something walked out of the sea at the lowest tide, and the tide took the colour with it (candidate a; ties to Tobin's line) |
| Emberfall highlanders | **Through trials:** trust earned side by side in hardship; bonds grow most from hard battles | The mountain's watcher failed, or slept, the night the colour went (candidate b, Toren's watcher) |
| Farwatch wanderers | **Brief and free:** travel with a creature for a while, then return it to the wild with a gift; closest in spirit to the Unbound | The bond itself burned too bright; some things should not be joined (candidate d; why wanderers understand the Unbound) |

**How it changes what you see and hear:** people of your own heritage speak more openly; some lines, signs and tales
read differently to you (a coastfolk tamer recognises the sea-song carving; a wanderer hears the Unbound's case
sympathetically); some clues are only noticed by one heritage, so a second journey with another heritage reveals
pieces the first couldn't.

**The roots.** Late in the story it emerges that all four heritages descend from **one older people who each witnessed a
different part of the same event** (the wild bond's fight): the farmers saw the aftermath on the land, the coast saw
what came from the sea, the highlanders saw the watcher, the wanderers saw the fused pair. Each tale is true as far as
it goes. Put together, they are the tapestry (docs/lore/wildbond-threads.md).

**Fair play.** Each bonding way is a full, strong way to play (no best choice); a heritage's tale is never the whole
truth; every heritage can reach the full truth, by different roads.

## Other games, where it fits

- **Realmbound:** its races and factions already exist. Each race keeps its own account of the Hollow Crown and what
  lies beneath it; your race changes who confides in you and which pieces you hear.
- **Otherworld:** your soul's origin, and each world's peoples remember the past differently; a life in one people can
  reveal what another hid.
- **Diamond Career:** a smaller version: your hometown and upbringing (small-town, academy, late starter) shape your first
  season's story and who believes in you.
