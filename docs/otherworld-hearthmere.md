# Hearthmere: Reedlight through winter (T39)
Codex, 2026-10-08. Branch codex/otherworld-hearthmere; review PR #63 after #62.

## The life
The player inherits the Reedlight Inn on a green lake. Vesper has kept its spare key; Tavi carries the last
lantern over the pass; Puddle lives in the well. Three authored mornings bring winter. Reading, leaving the
page and returning do not advance a day. Places are scene transitions like Asterhold, not a new walking system.
This is a compact complete authored life, not a claim that the proposed 20–40 minute systemic world is finished.

The first decisions open the door or repair the stove, earn Puddle's trust or draw practical water, and choose
where the last preparation day goes. Snow, pantry jars, firelight, guests, shutters and bindweed show the result.
The final choices explain plainly why an option is unavailable. Keeping a single warm room is always possible
and leads to a kind, smaller beginning rather than a game-over.

## Gifts and costs
- Hearth Cooking feeds and comforts guests but misses the dawn caravan. Tavi stays; the departure ending closes.
- Spirit Speech wakes the chimney spirit but worries neighbors. Visible work at the stove and step can mend this;
  otherwise Vesper makes an introduction in the epilogue. The mistrust is not a permanent penalty.
- Green Thumb fills the pantry but bindweed closes the step. Clearing it restores the cart route; if not, Vesper
  opens a side door and later cuts the growth back. The gift never creates a dead end.

Effects use existing entered-node receipts, so resuming the gift scene cannot repeat its supply gains or costs.
The new life.hearth counters have bounded defaults; they are local to this world. Old Asterhold fields, gift
amounts and endings are unchanged. No version bump, Godot edit, service-worker edit or new shared system.

## Four endings and carried knowledge
A Table Through Winter (hopeful) keeps broth and shelter; One Ember Kept (bittersweet) keeps shelter;
The Last Lantern Over the Pass (bittersweet) keeps broth; The Guest Beneath the Ice (strange) keeps thaw and shelter.
Every gift can reach the hopeful ending without old memories. Leaving requires packing in time with a clear
step. The spring promise needs Puddle's trust plus Spirit Speech or a carried song.

Broth opens a real new choice in Lanthorn's well queue, improving its food and fear with an authored response
from Ressa. The thaw song changes Bren's well scene and opens understanding in a later Hearthmere life.
Shelter is remembered at the inn door. Asterhold's rootsong can open Hearthmere's strange spring ending.
The Archivist has a distinct response to each memory. Memories are knowledge; no damage or stat bonuses.
