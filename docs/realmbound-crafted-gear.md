# Guild commissions (R5)

At level 55, a hero with a founded guild can speak to the Smith inside any Smithy and choose **Commission guild gear**. Patterns cover all eight equipment slots for all five classes. Browse three short pages, inspect the actual stats and full cost, then confirm. A refused commission shows the missing requirement and has no purchase button. Auto never chooses or pays for a commission.

## Equipment and economy

Every commission is a fixed item-level-55 rare with class-appropriate armour, the class's first supported weapon type, its off-hand type, and the existing generator's class stats. The five original families are Hearthguard (Warrior), Duskthread (Rogue), Emberscript (Mage), Hearthlight (Priest), and Trailwoven (Hunter). There are no random stats, new abilities or set bonuses. Crafted gear helps fill a weak slot; later dungeon rares, Heroic tiers and raid epics keep their higher item levels and rewards.

The Smith's coin fee is the item's existing vendor value × 1.25, rounded up. It is shown with the hero's current purse and guild's available supplies before confirmation. Reselling loses coin; commissioning cannot create a gathering-to-vendor profit loop. A full outfit takes 154 ore and 60 herbs plus the listed coin fees. At a level-55 worker's base gathering interval of about 253 seconds, that is about 10.8 worker-hours of Mining and 4.2 worker-hours of Herbalism, before guild-member mood multipliers; multiple guild workers can share the work. One smaller piece takes roughly an hour of Mining plus 17 minutes of Herbalism. Gathering's existing eight-hour uncollected cap stays intact. Nothing requires a daily visit.

The transaction rechecks level, founded guild, Smithy location, Focus mode, bag capacity, materials and money before any debit. Supplies come from the shared bank; money and the finished item belong only to the active hero. Equipment goes into bags, never auto-equips. No new account fields, crafting XP or permanent unlock tree; crafted items use the existing item save format plus an optional cosmetic provenance marker. Existing gear and older saves stay intact.
