# Realmbound regional hubs (R6)

Each existing zone keeps its hub and its road exit. Thornvale retains the familiar Abbey street; Red Sand bends around a caravan square; Fenwatch spreads over boardwalks and sheltered islands; Ashen puts its forge above the lower shelter; Lanternrest opens around a snowy lodge courtyard; Barrowfield has stone markers; the Hollow Crown has trees reclaiming its lanes; the Crown's Heart puts its resting house at the head of a flowering square. Concord buildings and Wildclan shelters use the existing faction art.

The Inn and Smithy are walk-in rooms. Speak to the Keeper to rest, or the Smith to sell scraps, repair equipment, or do both. Prices and recovery use the existing functions. Entering alone spends nothing. Auto keeps its existing smith-and-road visit without choosing conversations. Guild members still tell their stories only in the Guild Hall.

Original keepers, Inn / Smith: Thornvale Brother Cedran / Daven; Red Sand Orva / Korrin; Fenwatch Sella / Vask; Ashen Tannel / Breska; Lanternrest Heddra / Rulven; Barrowfield Maelin / Issar; Hollow Crown Vedra / Ferran; Crown's Heart Aster / Nolven. No quests or established factions change. Maps and room locations are runtime state; save format is unchanged.
