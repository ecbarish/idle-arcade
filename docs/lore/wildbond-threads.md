# Wildbond's threads: a story you sew together

Evan, 2026-10-08: weave the story so there are threads everywhere and we don't see the tapestry until the very end.
Unconnected pieces that don't make much sense at first; hints that build; misleads and twists that keep you guessing
instead of marching to an obvious conclusion. All the pieces are there, but you can't put them together properly until
further down the road. Guessable and knowable, yet, like Schrödinger's cat, anything could be possible.

**This page is for writers (Claude, ChatGPT, anyone).** Players never see it. Check it before writing any story line,
sign, dex entry, item text or NPC chatter in Wildbond, and add every clue you place.

## How we tell it

1. **Fair play.** Every clue needed for the truth is in the game before the reveal. A careful player can guess it; a
   second playthrough shows the clues were always there.
2. **Several truths stay possible.** Each mystery has two to four **candidate explanations**. Early clues are written so
   they fit more than one candidate. Only late clues narrow it down. The final truth must be chosen before its reveal
   is written, and must fit every clue already placed.
3. **Mislead through people, never through the game.** Characters can be wrong, biased, lying or half-remembering (the
   Unbound's founding account, Wardens with their own pride, old songs that changed in the telling). Narration, item
   text and the Wilddex never state something false.
4. **Small, strange and scattered.** A carving, a dex line that doesn't quite fit, a guardian who reacts oddly to one
   creature, a gap in a map, a phrase two unrelated people both use. Each makes sense alone; together they mean more.
5. **Payoffs at several sizes.** Small threads tie off early (so players learn that threads do pay off); big ones run
   the whole game; one or two stay open on purpose for later.
6. **Choices touch the threads.** What you do with the Unbound and the guardians changes which pieces you see and how
   the final picture lands, never whether the truth is reachable.

## The threads (living ledger)

For each: what players can see, the candidate explanations, the decided truth (if decided), and the clues placed.

### 1. The fading
- **Seen:** the valley lost its colour long ago; bonds bring it back; the guardians remember it as it was.
- **Decided core (Evan, 2026-10-08):** a wild bond fought something draining the land; the colour was lost because
  that fight was lost, or as a casualty of it.
- **Still open, keep several candidates alive:** what the bond fought. Candidates: **(a)** something from outside the
  valley that feeds on the land's memory; **(b)** a guardian that turned, or was turned; **(c)** people: an older order
  that took too much from the land (a mirror of the Unbound's fear, aimed the other way); **(d)** the bond itself went
  too far in winning (the Unbound would be half right). Clues should fit at least two of these until late.
- **Decided (Evan, 2026-10-08):** it took depth too; the valley is flat as well as grey. Fair early clues to plant: guardians
  describe "how deep the valley used to be"; old paintings in the inn look strangely "thick"; a creature that won't walk
  where a cliff "should" be. Depth returning late is a payoff, so seed it lightly from the first area.
- **Clues placed:** Isolde: "bonds help the world remember." Toren: warm stones beyond the high pass; a watcher older
  than the Wardens.

### 2. The wild bond
- **Seen:** a legend almost nobody believes; later, the player's own fusion (proposals/creature-catalogue-and-evolution.md §5).
- **Decided:** real; it is what the Unbound's founder saw.
- **Open:** who the tamer and creature in the old fight were, and what became of them (candidates: both lost; the
  creature survived as one of the guardians; the tamer became the first Warden; the pair is still "somewhere").

### 3. The Unbound
- **Seen:** a faction freeing creatures, opposing Wardens, with a founding text.
- **Decided:** the founder misread the fight. Some of their wider complaints are fair.
- **Open:** what the present leader really wants (candidates: sincere belief; using the cause for power; trying to
  finish what the old evil started without knowing it; trying to *prevent* it, badly).

### 4. Toren's watcher
- **Seen:** "something was keeping watch there long before we Wardens arrived."
- **Open:** candidates: the thing the wild bond fought, sleeping; the creature from the old wild bond; a guardian who
  failed to stop the fading and has kept watch in shame ever since.

### 5. The heritages' tales
- **Seen:** each heritage tells the fading differently (proposals/wildbond-heritage.md): tired land, something from the
  sea, the watcher who failed, a bond that burned too bright. Each matches one candidate for thread 1.
- **Decided (Evan, 2026-10-08):** all four descend from one older people who each witnessed a different part of the same
  event. Each tale is partly true. Some clues are only noticed with a given heritage.
- **Clues placed (Godot, 2026-10-08, main.gd HERITAGES):** told at the register. Farmfolk: the land grew tired after
  people took too much, and "one day it'll remember". Coastfolk: the tide went out further than ever and something
  walked out of the sea; the returning water took the colour. Highlanders: something kept watch inside the mountain and
  failed, or slept, that night. Wanderers: two things were joined that never should have been and burned so bright the
  world went grey. Maren: four tellings at one harvest supper; "they can't all be right; maybe they can't all be wrong".
  Tobin (to coastfolk) and the arrival lines at Saltmarsh, Emberfall and Cloudglass echo them.

### 6. Small threads to tie off early
- Veilmote, the reserved sprite that "folds deep shade around abandoned nests, keeping their last warmth": whose nests,
  and why does it guard what's left? (Could tie to thread 2 or 4.)
- Tobin's "Seen the tide go out further than it should, once. Didn't much like what walked out of it." (Breakwatermane,
  or something else first?)
