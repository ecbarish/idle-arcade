Wildbond · v1.3.0

The league, and beyond it

The story now ends: with all eight badges, the road north from Farwatch leads to the Returning Light League. After it, Champions can climb the Lighthouse Spire (levels 75 to 100) and rematch the league once a day. Stillreed also has a ferry landing you can walk onto, and the View button (or V) shows more of the world on big screens.

Have all eight badges? Take on the league

  1. Beat Wren at the gate, then the four courts in a row. Nelva heals your team between rooms; a loss keeps today's cleared rooms.
  2. Meet Champion Avenne. Win to see the homecoming and earn the Champion title.
  3. Walk west to the Lighthouse Spire. Climb as far as you like; every fifth floor is a rest where you can stop with your rewards.

Did the ending feel earned? Was the Spire a reason to keep raising your team? Tell us the floor you reached and how it felt.

On a big monitor?

Press V to switch between Close, Wide and Far. Does Wide or Far feel better on your screen?

Wildbond · v1.1.0

From a first partner to the horizon

The eighth area, Farwatch Reach, brings a coastal harbor, nine species, Watchlight and Warden Rysa's Horizon Badge. It is the last area before the league; the league is still to come in this release.

New here? Start with one partner

  1. Choose a partner in Larkhaven. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or tap a destination; use Enter to talk when you're beside someone.
  2. Head into Thornwood's grass. Try a battle and a catch, then find a place to rest.
  3. Open the Team, Ranch and Journal tabs. See whether you can find your partner, its care plan and where to go next.

Did the game make your next step clear? Did walking and choosing a move feel comfortable on your screen?

Already have the Loom Badge? Visit Farwatch

  1. Follow Sunthread Commons' coastal road into Farwatch Reach (levels 66–72). Explore the harbor and meet the two route trainers.
  2. Look for Wren's shared-notes rematch and Watchlight as you explore. Try the new encounters with the team you've raised.
  3. Meet Rysa and earn the eighth badge. The badge cap is 75 with all eight badges; check your team's cap note and the Journal afterward.

Does Farwatch feel like a place where a long journey pauses? Tell us if a path, trainer or level jump felt confusing or too steep.

Realmbound · v1.0.0

A hero, a town and a guild

The current build reaches level 60 and the Crown's Heart. Towns are walkable, and your guild can work, rest and join you in The Hollow Throne.

New here? Take a first quest

  1. Create a hero, talk to a quest giver and accept a quest. Try Focus combat, then Auto when available; notice what each asks you to do.
  2. Return to town. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or tap a destination, and visit the inn to heal.
  3. Open the Journal. Find your quest progress and the Export button; keep a copy of the exported text if you'd like a backup.

Could you tell when a quest was ready to turn in? Did the town help you find what your hero needed?

Already have a guild? Spend time in the hall

  1. Visit the guild hall. Talk with an adventurer and read their mood and favor.
  2. Try a job on the guild's board, then return to see its progress and the supply bank. Check that the result makes sense for the job.
  3. At level 60, with the Hollow Key earned through the Crown's Heart quests, try gathering a raid team for The Hollow Throne. This is an optional longer route.

Do guild members feel useful and present? If a job or raid gate is unclear, include the message you saw and your hero's level.

A quick comfort check

Try your usual screen

On a phone, can you read a dialogue and tap a choice without pinching to zoom? On a desktop or ultrawide, are the scene and text easy to see? Name your device and browser if something feels cramped or tiny.

Return to your save

After a quiet moment outside a battle, close and reopen the game. Did your progress and chosen settings return? Don't reset your existing adventure just to test; a separate browser profile can give you a fresh start.

Saves live in this browser. Keep any backup text somewhere safe. This notes page does not read or change your saves.
